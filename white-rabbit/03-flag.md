# White Rabbit - Flag

**Captured via:** Jenkins `PR-1` build console output (`Debug Env` stage), base64-decoded locally.

**Flag:** `FLAG{...redacted...}`

The flag is intentionally redacted here since this is a public repo. CICD-Goat flags are
shared across everyone who runs the lab, so this isn't a real secret, but it's still
good practice not to publish it verbatim. The extraction method (base64 encoding to
bypass Jenkins' credential log masking) is documented in full in `02-attack.md`, which
is the part that actually demonstrates the skill.
